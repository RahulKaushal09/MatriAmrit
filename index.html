The MatriAmrit vision

We believe that motherhood is sacred, and every child deserves the purest beginning in life. Rooted in the timeless wisdom of Ayurveda and Sanatana Dharma, we support mothers and nurture newborns through every stage of their journey — from wholesome postpartum nutrition and natural baby foods to guidance on traditional Samskaras, infant care and holistic well-being.

Our vision extends beyond the well-being of mothers and children. Through simple, practical, sustainable daily practices inspired by Sanatana Dharma, we help families cultivate physical health, mental peace and spiritual well-being — while maintaining harmony with modern life.

Care, Stage by Stage

Every phase of motherhood asks for something different.

Ayurveda does not treat pregnancy, sutika kala and weaning as one long blur. Neither do we. Choose where you are — we will show you what your body, and your baby, need right now.

Stage 01 — Before conception

Preparing the soil before planting the seed.

गर्भाधान संस्कार · Garbhadhana

Classical Ayurveda is unambiguous: the quality of shukra and artava determines the child's constitution. Preconception care is not optional — it is the first Samskara.

  • Three-month shodhana and diet reset for both partners
  • Folate, iron and B12 from whole foods, not just tablets
  • Cycle-tracking guidance grounded in ritucharya
  • Mantra, meditation and sleep hygiene for both parents
See preconception essentials

“A seed sown in ready earth needs no coaxing.”

Charaka Samhita · Sharira Sthana

Stage 02 — Nine months

Month-by-month nourishment, the way Garbhini Paricharya prescribes.

गर्भिणी परिचर्या · Garbhini Paricharya

Ayurveda gives a distinct dietary regimen for each month of pregnancy. Our pregnancy laddu and daily guidance follow that calendar — sweet and cooling early, grounding and iron-rich later.

  • Pregnancy Laddu — dates, almond, ghee, gentle spice
  • Trimester-wise meal charts & nausea-safe options
  • Garbha Samvad — prenatal bonding, music and mantra
  • Punsavana & Simantonnayana ritual guidance
See pregnancy range

“Each month asks for a different food.”

Sushruta Samhita · month-wise regimen

Stage 03 — The first 42 days

The window that decides the next forty years.

सूतिका काल · Sutika Kala

Ayurveda treats the 42 days after birth as a distinct clinical period. Vata is high, agni is low, tissues are depleted. Warm, unctuous, easily digested food is not indulgence — it is treatment.

  • Postpartum Laddu with gond, methi, dry ginger & bilona ghee
  • A 42-day meal plan for uterine involution and Vata pacification
  • Abhyanga oil & binding practice, explained safely
  • Honest support for mood, sleep debt and identity shift
See postpartum care

Milk is made of what the mother eats, rests on and feels.

स्तन्य वर्धन · Stanya Vardhana

Stanyajanana herbs — shatavari, jeera, saunf, methi — have been used for galactagogue support for centuries. We pair them with the hydration, calories and rest protocol that actually makes them work.

  • Lactation Laddu with shatavari, fennel & jeera
  • Supply troubleshooting written by IBCLC-trained counsellors
  • Night-feed nutrition that doesn't wreck your digestion
  • Gentle weaning roadmap when you're ready

The first grain is a ceremony, not a chore.

अन्नप्राशन संस्कार · Annaprashana

Annaprashana marks a child's first taste of solid food. We help you hold the ritual properly — and then supply the cereal that follows it for the next eighteen months.

Sixteen Rites of Passage

The Samskaras, explained without superstition.

Each Samskara marks a real developmental threshold. We give you the meaning, the timing, the materials list and a plain-language script — so you can hold the ceremony yourself, at home, without a 40-page manual.

01

Garbhadhana

गर्भाधान

Conception approached consciously — the intention, diet and state of mind of both parents before a child is conceived.

Before conception

02

Punsavana

पुंसवन

A third-month rite for the healthy formation of the foetus, traditionally paired with specific herbal preparations.

Month 3 of pregnancy

03

Simantonnayana

सीमन्तोन्नयन

The Vedic baby shower — parting the mother's hair, offering her comfort, and settling her mind for the final trimester.

Month 7 of pregnancy

04

Jatakarma

जातकर्म

Performed at birth: honey and ghee on the tongue, the whispering of mantras, and the father's first welcome to the child.

At birth

05

Namakarana

नामकरण

The naming ceremony — held on the eleventh or twelfth day, choosing a name by nakshatra, meaning and family tradition.

Day 11–12

06

Nishkramana

निष्क्रमण

The child's first outing — first sunlight, first air, first sight of the sky, usually in the fourth month.

Month 4

07

Annaprashana

अन्नप्राशन

First solid food. A ceremony of gratitude to anna before the long relationship between a child and their plate begins.

Month 6–8

08

Chudakarana

चूडाकरण

The first haircut, or mundan — traditionally in the first or third year, marking a shedding of the past.

Year 1–3

09

Karnavedha

कर्णवेध

Ear-piercing, described in Sushruta Samhita for both protection and ornament — with clear modern hygiene guidance.

Month 6 – Year 5

10

Vidyarambha

विद्यारम्भ

The beginning of learning — the child's first letters, traced in rice or sand, before formal schooling starts.

Year 3–5

11

Upanayana

उपनयन

Initiation into study and discipline — historically the threshold into a life of learning under a teacher.

Year 5–8

12

…and five more

शेष संस्कार

Vedarambha, Keshanta, Samavartana, Vivaha and Antyeshti complete the sixteen — the full arc of a human life.
