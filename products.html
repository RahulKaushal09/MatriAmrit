Sourcing, In Full

If we can't name the farm, we don't use the ingredient.

Supplier names, certificates and batch lab reports are shared with any customer who asks. This is the short version.

Ghee & Dairy

A single grass-fed Gir cow dairy in Gujarat. Cultured curd, hand-churned bilona, never centrifuged cream. Batch-tested for adulterants.

Herbs & Gond

Shatavari, bala and gond katira from a GMP-certified Ayurvedic supplier in Rajasthan, with species verification on every consignment.

Millets & Grains

Ragi, bajra and moong from a farmer collective in northern Karnataka. Sprouted in-house, sun-dried, then stone-ground within 48 hours.

Nuts & Sweeteners

Kashmiri almonds and walnuts, Rajasthani dates, and organic sugarcane jaggery. No refined sugar, no glucose syrup, no palm-derived fat.
