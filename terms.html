Section 08Cancellation & refund policy

Our products are freshly prepared perishable foods intended for pregnant women, new mothers and infants. For food-safety reasons we cannot accept the return of a delivered food product, and we cannot resell anything that has left our premises. The policy below is written to be fair within that constraint.

Cancelling an order

  • Before dispatch: you may cancel any order free of charge by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing care@matriamrit.in with your order number. We refund 100% of the amount paid.
  • After dispatch: orders cannot be cancelled, because the food has already been made and shipped.
  • Cancelled by us: if we cancel your order for any reason in Section 05, you receive a full refund including shipping.

When we replace or refund

We will replace the item free of charge, or refund it in full, in these cases:

  • You received the wrong product or the wrong quantity.
  • The product arrived damaged, leaking, or with a broken tamper seal.
  • The product was expired or past its best-before date on arrival.
  • An item listed in your invoice was missing from the parcel.
  • The shipment is confirmed lost in transit by the courier.

How to raise a claim

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp (+91 78384 41441) or at care@matriamrit.in.
  2. Send your order number, a photograph of the outer packaging, the product, and the batch number and best-before date printed on the pack.
  3. Please keep the product and its packaging until the claim is resolved.
  4. We investigate and respond within 3 working days, and tell you whether we are replacing the item or refunding it.

What is not eligible

  • Change of mind, or the taste, texture, aroma or sweetness not matching your expectation.
  • Natural batch-to-batch variation in colour, size or firmness, or softening of ghee- or jaggery-based products in warm weather.
  • Products that have been opened, partly consumed, or stored contrary to the instructions on the pack, except where the complaint is a genuine quality or safety defect.

  • Claims raised more than 48 hours after delivery.
  • Failed delivery caused by an incorrect address, an unreachable phone number, or repeated refusal to accept the parcel.
